Expense and Savings Tracker

Expense and Savings Tracker is a Windows and Android application developed by Nandakumar M for recording everyday income and expenses, planning monthly budgets, tracking savings, and reviewing loan and EMI schedules.

Keep your plan on your device, or choose account sign-in and cloud synchronization to use the same plan across your devices.

Read the privacy policy Contact app support

Your day-to-day finances, in one plan

  • Record and edit dated income and expense entries by category.
  • Set monthly budgets and compare planned amounts with your actual entries.
  • Review savings forecasts, loan schedules, and EMI payments.
  • Create local backups and export reports to Excel, CSV, or PDF.

Use it locally or across devices

Local tracking works without an account. When you choose to sign in and enable synchronization, the app stores your plan in Supabase under your account. The app asks how to link a device and presents conflicting edits for review.

Why Google sign-in?

Google sign-in identifies your account so you can access your synchronized plan. The app requests basic profile and email information; it does not request access to Gmail messages, Google Drive files, contacts, or bank accounts. Google handles your Google password.

This website introduces the installed application. It does not provide a browser version of the tracker or ask you to enter financial records here.
