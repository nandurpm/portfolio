Savings and Expense Tracker

Savings and Expense Tracker is a web, Windows, and Android application developed by Nandakumar M for recording everyday income and expenses, planning monthly budgets, tracking savings, and reviewing loan and EMI schedules.

Use the browser app with sign-in, or install the Windows or Android app for local tracking with optional account synchronization.

Your day-to-day finances, in one plan

  • Record and edit dated income and expense entries by category.
  • Set monthly budgets and compare planned amounts with your actual entries.
  • Review savings forecasts, loan schedules, and EMI payments.
  • Create local backups and export reports to Excel, CSV, or PDF.

Use it locally or across devices

In the installed Windows and Android apps, local tracking works without an account. The browser app requires sign-in. When you choose to sign in and enable synchronization, the app stores your plan in Supabase under your account. The app asks how to link a device and presents conflicting edits for review.

Why Google sign-in?

Google sign-in identifies your account so you can access your synchronized plan. The app requests basic profile and email information; it does not request access to Gmail messages, Google Drive files, contacts, or bank accounts. Google handles your Google password.

The browser tracker is available on this website. It requires sign-in before displaying the financial workspace. Protect your browser profile and exported backups, especially on shared devices.
