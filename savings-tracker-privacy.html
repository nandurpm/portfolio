Expense and Savings Tracker

Privacy policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

This policy explains how Expense and Savings Tracker, developed and operated by Nandakumar M, handles information in its Windows and Android applications. It covers local tracking, optional account sign-in, and cloud synchronization.

1. Information the app handles

  • Financial records you enter: income, expenses, dates, categories, notes, budgets, savings settings, and loan or EMI details.
  • Account information: your account identifier and email address when you sign in. If you choose Google sign-in, Google may also supply basic profile information such as your name and profile image as part of authentication.
  • Session and synchronization information: authentication tokens, account-to-device linking information, plan revisions, and synchronization timestamps needed to keep you signed in and synchronize your plan.
  • Local settings and history: preferences, change history, and backup files saved on your device.

The app does not connect to a bank or automatically read bank transactions. It does not request access to Gmail messages, Google Drive files, contacts, or Google Calendar. Google sign-in requests basic email and profile scopes only. Your Google password is entered with Google, not supplied to the tracker.

2. How the information is used

Records are used to display your transactions, calculate budgets and forecasts, generate reports, and restore backups. Account and Google user information are used to authenticate you and associate your synchronized plan with the correct account. Session information supports secure sign-in; revision information supports synchronization and conflict review.

The app does not include advertising or behavioral analytics integrations. Google user data and financial records are not sold or used for advertising.

3. Where information is stored and shared

Local use: your plan is stored in the app's local files on your device. An account is not required for local tracking. Local backups and exported reports remain in the locations you select or the app's backup storage.

Optional cloud sync: after you sign in and choose to link and synchronize a plan, its financial records and relevant plan settings are sent to Supabase for storage and synchronization across your signed-in devices. Google sign-in itself does not upload your financial plan to Google.

Service providers: Google processes Google authentication, and Supabase processes account authentication and synchronized records. These services may process technical information such as IP addresses, request times, and service logs to operate and secure their services. The website host also processes normal website requests. See Google's Privacy Policy and Supabase's Privacy Policy.

The developer and service providers may need to access relevant information to provide requested support, administer the service, investigate security issues, or comply with applicable legal requirements. Exported files are shared only when you choose to send or store them elsewhere.

4. Google API user data

The app uses Google account information only for the sign-in and account features described in this policy. It does not transfer Google user data to advertising platforms, data brokers, or AI training services. Its use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements where applicable.

5. Your choices and deletion

  • Use local tracking without signing in or enabling cloud sync.
  • Edit or delete individual records in the app. When sync is enabled, these changes are synchronized to your account.
  • Export a backup before removing data or changing devices.
  • Sign out in the app, and remove its connection from your Google Account connections to revoke Google access. Signing out or revoking access does not itself delete a cloud plan.
  • Request deletion of your app account and cloud plan by emailing nandhakumardesign.org@gmail.com from the registered email address. Identify that the request concerns Expense and Savings Tracker. Ownership may need to be verified; never send passwords or sign-in codes.

6. Retention and backups

Local records remain until you remove or replace them or clear the app's local data. Uninstall behavior depends on the platform; exported files and separate backups may remain after uninstalling. You control copies you have exported.

Account and synchronized records remain in the cloud while the account and plan are retained, until deleted through the app's record controls or a verified deletion request. Service logs and provider backup copies can be retained under the providers' operational retention processes or where required by law. Deleting a cloud record does not erase independent local backups or exports on your devices.

7. Security

Online authentication and synchronization use HTTPS. Cloud access is associated with your signed-in account. Local files and exports can contain sensitive financial details: protect your device and backups, and share exports only with people you intend. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Contact Nandakumar M at nandhakumardesign.org@gmail.com for app privacy questions, support, or deletion requests.
