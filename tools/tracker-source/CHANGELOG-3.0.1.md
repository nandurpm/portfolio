## Savings and Expense Tracker 3.0.1

- Imports quoted decimal-comma amounts and validates digit grouping before applying CSV or Excel entries.
- Shows effective budgets in Excel and preserves saved overrides and account assignments when reimporting the workbook.
- Clears stale account records when explicitly restoring an older backup without account data.
- Recognizes recorded full loan settlements in net worth and compares the lender quote with the remaining scheduled payments.
- Shows refunds, reversals, and recorded transactions in inactive categories in the monthly checklist.
- Prevents a new purchase reversal from taking effect before the linked refund reversal.
